`expo-ble-kit/compat/plx`: react-native-ble-plx 3.5.1's API (`BleManager`, `Device`, `Service`, `Characteristic`, `Descriptor`, `BleError` with its numeric codes and messages, base64 values, transaction ids) over expo-ble-kit, so an app moves by changing its imports. The differences are listed in the new migration guide, `docs/guides/migration-from-plx.md`.
